# TypePress

A free t shirt design generator for typography designs. Paste your phrases, pick a style, and download print
ready transparent PNGs in bulk.

**Use it online, nothing to download:** https://notzeroyet.com/typepress/

**Video tutorial:** [Watch the TypePress walkthrough on YouTube](https://youtu.be/VLDTeq4Kgno)

## Open your own copy

The whole tool is one file, `index.html`. There's nothing to install and no account to make.

1. On this page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip it. On a Mac, double click the ZIP file. On Windows, right click it and choose **Extract All**.
3. Open the unzipped folder and double click `index.html`. It opens in your web browser.

It works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, on Mac, Windows, Linux and Chromebook. On a Chromebook, open
the file from the Files app. On a phone, use the website instead, since phones don't open files from a ZIP
very well.

The first time you open it you need an internet connection so the fonts can load. After that the tool runs
in your browser on your own computer.

## How to use it

1. Pick a style: **1 · Text**, **2 · Graphic**, **3 · Pattern**, **4 · Block** or **5 · Symbol**.
2. Paste your designs into the text field. A blank line starts a new design, and each line of a design is a
   line on the shirt.
3. Pick a font, ink color and layout. The preview updates as you go.
4. Press **Generate**, then **Download Zip** for everything, or **PNG** on a single design.

A few shortcuts inside the text field:

- `*word*` puts a word in the accent color.
- `_word_` underlines a word.
- A line that starts with `- ` becomes a small sub line, like "(respectfully)".
- In the Graphic style, a line with only `---` splits the top text from the bottom text.

## What you get

Every design comes as a pair: your ink color plus a soft white version for dark shirts (white ink pairs with
black instead). Files are 4500 x 5400 pixel transparent PNGs at 300 DPI, never pure black or pure white, which
print better.

## Fonts

Every font TypePress loads is a Google Font under the SIL Open Font License, which allows commercial printing.
If you upload your own font or pattern, you need your own license for it.

## Your data

Your designs are made in your own browser, on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
